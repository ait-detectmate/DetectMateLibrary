# Security Policy

## Supported Versions

| Version | Supported          |
| ------- | ------------------ |
| 1.x.x   | :white_check_mark: |
| < 1.0.0 | :x:                |

> [!IMPORTANT]
> DetectMateLibrary is under active development. You can report potential vulnerabilities through the usual issue tracker. [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/ait-detectmate/DetectMateLibrary/issues/)

## Reporting a Vulnerability

Please email reports of security-related issues to aecid@ait.ac.at. A small team of developers receives these reports. We will acknowledge your email within one business day and send a detailed response with the next steps within seven days.

Please use a descriptive subject line. After the initial reply, our team will endeavor to keep you informed as we work on a fix and an announcement.

Please include the following information in your report.

* Your name and affiliation (if any).
* The technical details of the vulnerability, including steps to reproduce your findings.
* An explanation of who could exploit the vulnerability and what they could gain. An attack scenario helps us evaluate complex reports quickly.
* Whether the vulnerability is public or known to third parties, along with any relevant details.
* Whether we may credit you in the changelog.

After receiving a report, we follow this disclosure process.

* When a report is received, we confirm the issue and determine its severity.
* If we know of specific third-party services or software based on DetectMateService that require mitigation before publication, those projects will be notified.
* Fixes are prepared for the last minor release of the latest major release.
* We publish patch releases for all affected versions that receive fixes.
